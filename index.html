About

Currently doing an intership at CSEM on robot manipulation. I work with two UR7e arms and Robotiq 2F-85 grippers on cable insertion where millimetre tolerances make every attempt a small physics problem.

My research interests are in robot learning: imitation learning with vision-language-action (VLA) models, world action models (WAMs) that learn how the world responds to the robot's actions, reinforcement learning to push policies beyond what demonstrations alone can teach and dexterous manipulation.

My current pipeline goes from teleoperated demonstrations on the real robot, to fine-tuning π0.5 VLA model. I'm interested in what it takes for these models to work reliably on contact-rich tasks, and in the engineering that gets them there.

Before CSEM I was a researcher at Politecnico di Torino where I built a synthetic dataset for anomaly segmentation. I also did my M.Sc. at Politecnico di Torino in Computer Engineering and my B.Sc. at Università Degli Studi di Torino in Computer Science. I live in Luzern, Switzerland.
